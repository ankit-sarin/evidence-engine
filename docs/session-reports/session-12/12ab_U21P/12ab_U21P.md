## §6 Findings

**F1.** The calibration rule worked as designed: a request too short to observe stopped the probe before any variant, with the lock released, the model unloaded and confirmed, and no restart.

**F2.** The prompt wording was the probe's and it failed: `llama3.2:3b` declined. A re-run needs a different instruction and a ruling; this brief says "no variant is re-sent".

**F3.** `done_reason: stop` at 147 tokens is the model ending its own answer, so `num_predict` was never reached and `num_ctx` never mattered.
