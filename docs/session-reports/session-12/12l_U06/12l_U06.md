## What changed

- **The floor** (`engine/core/extraction_events.py`): `held_count`, `expected_count`, `below_floor`, `floor_failure`. Held = an asserted value (a cited sentinel is a value) or a declined outcome; `contract_unmet` and a field that never came back are not held. A record holding fewer than half of the expected fields plans as `extraction_failed` / `held_fields_below_floor` with no claim. A record with no field at all keeps `no_fields_returned`.
  - `plan_extraction_events` applies it to every record.
  - `write_extraction_events` refuses a record under the floor with `HeldFieldsBelowFloor` and writes nothing. A success-path caller therefore cannot go on as if it had stored an extraction; this is how the elicited path's un-exhausted record reaches the failure branch without `pipeline.py` being edited.
  - `outcome_for_exception` returns the floor's `PaperFailure` for such a record, whether it came on a refusal (exhausted) or from the write boundary.
  - `counts_toward_abort` uses the same predicate (N09).
- **The failure's detail** (the paper event's payload): held, expected, asserted, declined, contract_unmet, the unmet fields' violation codes, the incomplete fields, attempts, the extraction uid and the context chain. None of the record's claims is stored.
- **The attempt rule** (`extractor.extract_paper_with_completeness`): across attempts the refusal whose record holds the most fields is kept; a later one replaces it only if it holds strictly more; an attempt with no record (an unparseable response) never replaces one. `HeldFieldsBelowFloor` is recorded in telemetry (`outcome: below_floor`) and re-raised, not retried.
- **Readers of `incomplete_fields`**: `extraction_events.run_extraction_summary` adds `stored_incomplete`, `incomplete_fields`, `all_declined` and `below_floor` to the run's stats and logs them; `prisma.generate_prisma_flow` adds `extracted_incomplete` and `incomplete_fields`, and the CSV gains an "Extracted with incomplete fields" row with one line per field when the count is non-zero.
- **INT-l12-18** (`engine/core/events.py`): `_refuse_claim_on_arm` raises `ClaimOnUnpinnedArm` for a claim on a model arm whose row is not `pinned`, naming the arm.
- **Riding:** the driver's log line and docstring, `completeness.py`'s module docstring and `UncitedValueError`'s docstring now say what is stored (Wave 1 rows 55 item 1, 83 (a) and (d)); `paper_state.py`'s comment on the reason vocabulary states the floor.
- **Not changed:** `engine/agents/audit_events.py` (in the brief's list; nothing of the floor reaches it).
