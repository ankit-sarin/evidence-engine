## §7 Limits

- **Five cases, one call per arm.** The data bound the template's effect; they do not measure it.
  At temperature 0 identical requests already gave different responses in 12r-U13-PROBE (calls 1
  against 2–3) and here: the 12g_p8 warm-up and its scored old call, the same request, differ in text
  (1,128 vs 1,200 chars) though both are stated_only.
- **The old arm reproduced the historical shape on 3 of 5.** So run-to-run variation is of the same
  order as the effect looked for (CC's inference).
- **Prompt drift for 12g:** the 12g cases' attempt-1 prompt is today's, not 12g's (§1).
- **Server state:** one warm load; every scored call followed another call on a resident model. 12g's
  and 12r's historical attempt 2 followed their own attempt 1 on the same paper.

## §8 Findings

- **F1** — Five of the six STATED-only-feedback cases were replayable; the September case is not, because its attempt-1 response was never stored.
- **F2** — The old-template rebuild reproduces both stored attempt-2 request hashes (12r_p8 `e14ca615…7658`, 12r_p12 `91016de9…acec`), and on every case the new request differs from the old only by R-1's 510 characters and R-2's 217.
- **F3** — STATED-only re-emission was 2 of 5 on the old template and 1 of 5 on the new, against 4 of 5 historically for the same cases.
- **F4** — One case moved from STATED-only to a full, contract-met response under the new template (12g_p10); none moved the other way.
- **F5** — 12g_p8 (live paper 455) re-emitted STATED-only under both templates and on the warm-up, three of three, with the whole schema named in the new arm's header.
- **F6** — The old template reproduced the historical shape on only 3 of 5 cases, so single calls at temperature 0 do not separate the template's effect from run-to-run variation on this sample.
- **F7** — The new template adds 173 prompt tokens per attempt-2 request on every case.
- **F8** — The window held one foreign read-only `ollama ps` (18:00:29) and no foreign load; no NVRM line, no earlyoom kill, NRestarts 0, nothing resident after the unload.
