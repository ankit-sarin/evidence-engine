Papers describing bench/preclinical autonomous tasks (e.g., suturing, knot-tying on phantoms) are included regardless of the specialty context, as these represent generalizable autonomous capabilities. Orthopedic and neurosurgery papers are excluded only when the autonomous task is specialty-specific (e.g., autonomous bone drilling, stereotactic targeting). If the paper describes a generalizable autonomous task performed in an orthopedic or neurosurgical context, include it. When in doubt between INCLUDE and EXCLUDE, lean toward INCLUDE — downstream full-text screening will catch false positives. If the abstract gives too little information to decide, choose EXCLUDE — do not default to inclusion when evidence is absent.
