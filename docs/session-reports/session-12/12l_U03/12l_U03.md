## I1, I2

- **I1 does not hold as worded.** The brief: "For a JUDGMENT field the step-level unit indices are not in the record's field index list." They are. `engine/elicitation/contracts.py::check_entry`: `cited: list[int] = list(idx)` → `for s in steps: for i in s.indices: if i not in cited: cited.append(i)` → `FieldRecord(… indices=cited_t, … steps=steps …)`. So `FieldRecord.indices` is the field-level indices followed by the steps', de-duplicated, and each `Step.indices` holds that step's own. `pipeline.py` counts citations as `citation_counts[name] = len(rec.indices)`. The record is built to D-d either way: `cited` is `record.indices` (every index the citation check counts) and `steps` keeps each step's indices.
- **I2 holds.** `plan_extraction_events`: `source_snippet=fo.source_snippet if fo.kind == VALUE else None` under `event_type=_EVENT_TYPE[fo.kind]`, so of the extractor's claim events only `asserted` carries a snippet; `contract_unmet` and `declined` carry none. A cited absence sentinel is a value: `terminal.terminal_states` gives every record that is `ok` and not the escape token `C.EVIDENCED_VALUE`, and `elicited_record` makes it a VALUE outcome. Every `asserted` event the elicited path writes carries the record. The legacy path's `asserted` events also carry a snippet, typed by the model; they cite no unit and carry no record (F3).

## The record as implemented

One payload key, `citation_record`, declared in `engine/core/events.py` as `PAYLOAD_CITATION_RECORD`, built by `engine/elicitation/materialize.py::citation_record`, carried on `FieldOutcome.citation_record`, passed through `elicited_record(citation_records=…)` and written by `plan_extraction_events` on a VALUE.

- `units_version` — the segmentation the indices belong to (`units-2`).
- `cited` — `FieldRecord.indices`, in the model's order.
- `runs` — the runs derived from `cited` (sorted, de-duplicated), each with `first`, `last` (unit indices) and `start`, `end` (character offsets into the parsed text the claim names by `parsed_text_sha256`). `runs[0]` is the stored `source_snippet`.
- `steps` — JUDGMENT only, one entry per step in order: its `cited`, its `runs`, and `criteria_application`.

Every run, field-level and step-level, goes through the same EXACT check as the snippet (`_run_slice`; `SnippetNotExact` otherwise). Offsets index the decoded text, not its bytes.

A STATED claim citing `[47, 48, 12]` (the test's paper):

```json
{"units_version": "units-2", "cited": [47, 48, 12],
 "runs": [{"first": 12, "last": 12, "start": 533, "end": 581},
          {"first": 47, "last": 48, "start": 2211, "end": 2306}]}
```

A JUDGMENT claim citing unit 5 at field level and `[20, 21, 9]`, `[30]` and a criteria application in three steps:

```json
{"units_version": "units-2", "cited": [5, 20, 21, 9, 30],
 "runs": [{"first": 5, "last": 5, "start": 204, "end": 249}, {"first": 9, "last": 9, "start": 391, "end": 440},
          {"first": 20, "last": 21, "start": 910, "end": 1006}, {"first": 30, "last": 30, "start": 1399, "end": 1445}],
 "steps": [{"cited": [20, 21, 9], "runs": [{"first": 9, "last": 9, "start": 391, "end": 440},
                                            {"first": 20, "last": 21, "start": 910, "end": 1006}], "criteria_application": false},
           {"cited": [30], "runs": [{"first": 30, "last": 30, "start": 1399, "end": 1445}], "criteria_application": false},
           {"cited": [], "runs": [], "criteria_application": true}]}
```

- **F1 — `cited` is the model's order with repeats removed, and a JUDGMENT field's field-level list is not separable (I1).** `contracts._resolve_indices` drops a repeated index before `FieldRecord` is built (the repeat is the advisory `DUPLICATE_INDICES`, in telemetry only), and `check_entry` merges field-level and step indices into one list.
