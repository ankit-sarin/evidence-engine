## §7 Findings, for ruling

- **F1** — The cause on papers 3 and 10 is **the model's output**: each attempt-1 response is complete,
  parsing JSON that ends with `stop` and names STATED fields only (§4: "present `stated 8/9, inferable
  0/6, judgment 0/5`", "`9/0/0`", `done_reason: stop`). Not the checker or the parser (the names come
  from the raw text, and the re-check reproduces the recorded codes); not a cut-short response. Which
  change in the request moved the model there is **not decidable from this read**: the numbered text
  differs on both papers (U01) and the instruction text differs by the three added sentences and one
  reworded clause of §3, at temperature 0 on the same model digest.
- **F2** — The shape is not new: 12g produced it three times, all on attempt 2 after feedback that
  named only STATED fields (`"attempt2_stated_only": ["8", "9", "10"]`). 12r produced it twice on
  attempt 1 and once on attempt 2 (`"attempt1_stated_only": ["3", "10"]`, `"attempt2_stated_only":
  ["8"]`). Because 12h's comparison scores attempt 1 only, the 12g occurrences never entered its
  counts.
- **F3** — Attempt 2 after STATED-only feedback returned STATED-only on 4 of 5 attempts across the two
  runs (§5). The feedback block's own words ask for every field ("re-emit EVERY field in the
  schema").
- **F4** — F5 of 12r_U13.md is two different things: paper 8's attempt 2 is this STATED-only shape;
  paper 9's attempt 2 answered every field and failed every INFERABLE inference (`INFERENCE_MISSING 6`).
- **F5** — The prompt is reproducible from the tree: HEAD's builders reproduce 12r's stored attempt-1
  request hash 4 of 4, 90f4697's reproduce 12g's stored hash 3 of 3 checkable, and HEAD's feedback
  re-render reproduces 12r's attempt-2 hash 4 of 4 (§2, §3).
- **F6** — `num_predict` is neither sent nor recorded, and `num_ctx` is `unset` / `modelfile_or_server`
  on both runs (§2). Not a cause here (`stop` throughout), recorded because INT-g12-6's output
  headroom question rests on it.
- **F7** — 12g's per-attempt `done_reason` is not in its telemetry but is in its run.log: its three
  STATED-only attempts ended `"done_reason": "stop"` (§4), so they were not cut short; with no raw text
  stored, A rather than B is CC's inference for them.
- **F8** — R667(b) puts the INT-h12-2 note in this Phase A's commit, but this brief writes only under
  `~/scratch/12r-u13-pa/` and this directory, and `prefix_check.py` checks only the INT-p12-2 row (R664(c)).
  The note's text is in §8 and the plan is not edited; the note lands where the next ruling says.
