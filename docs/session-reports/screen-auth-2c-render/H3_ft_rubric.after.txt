FT_ELIGIBLE only if every inclusion criterion holds:
  - English language
  - Peer-reviewed original research (journal article or full conference paper)
  - Involves a physical surgical task (cadaver, phantom, animal, or human)
  - Must involve an autonomous or semi-autonomous robotic component that CONTROLS or DIRECTS a physical robot to execute a surgical action (not purely teleoperated or manual)
  - Simulation studies included ONLY IF they demonstrate or evaluate autonomous/semi-autonomous robotic behavior in the simulation (not simulation frameworks, physics engines, or virtual environments described without autonomous robot behavior)
  - Published between 2010 and 2025
FT_SCREENED_OUT if any exclusion criterion applies (the bracketed code names the reason):
  - [review_article] Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or scoping reviews
  - [editorial_commentary] Editorials, commentaries, or letters to the editor
  - [abstract_only] Conference abstracts without full paper
  - [wrong_intervention] Non-surgical robotics (industrial, warehouse, rehabilitation, exoskeletons, prosthetics, assistive devices)
  - [teleoperation_only] Purely teleoperated/master-slave robotic surgery where the human directly controls all robot movements in real-time (e.g., standard da Vinci case series, joystick-controlled systems). There must be an autonomous or semi-autonomous component where the system makes independent decisions about motion, planning, or execution.
  - [analysis_only] ML/CV methodology papers that only analyze, classify, segment, track, or predict surgical data (video, images, sensor data) without controlling a robot to execute a surgical action (e.g., planning-only papers — surgical motion, path, or task planning computed from data without controlling a robot to execute it)
  - [no_autonomy_content] Studies with no autonomous or semi-autonomous component (e.g., hardware, sensor, or instrument papers that describe a robot or device without autonomous control)
  - [insufficient_data] Papers with no abstract or insufficient information to determine eligibility — do not default to inclusion when evidence is absent

SPECIALTY SCOPE [wrong_specialty] — Excluded: dental surgery, oral and maxillofacial surgery, ophthalmic surgery, orthopedic surgery, dermatologic surgery, neurosurgery
