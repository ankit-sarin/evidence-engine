## Answer

**Concentrated — prompt content.** And, separately, **the retry is not simply doing its job** on the 12g sample.

1. **First-attempt failures are concentrated in two places, and each has a sentence in the prompt behind it.**
   On `ra_12g`, 46 of 220 first-attempt fields failed. 37 of the 46 sit in 88 field-attempts: the three STATED
   fields whose own codebook instruction says to record `NR` (15 of 33 failed, 45%) and the five JUDGMENT fields
   (22 of 55, 40%). The other 132 field-attempts carry 9 failures (7%).
2. **The retry repairs STATED fields and damages INFERABLE ones.** Across the ten `ra_12g` papers that ran a
   second attempt, it fixed 39 of the 46 failing fields and broke 55 fields that had passed. Three second
   responses contained no INFERABLE or JUDGMENT entry at all. The acceptance rule kept every net-worse second
   attempt out: it was accepted on 4 of 10 papers. Why second responses drop whole classes is **undetermined**:
   the rejected attempt's raw response is not on disk.
3. **The September smokes do not show the damage.** Under the same feedback template, four paper-runs went from
   22 failing fields to 4, and the second attempt was accepted on all four.

The brief made no prediction for this read.

### The Pass-1 text that states the citation contract

`engine/elicitation/prompts.py`. The system message, `SYSTEM_PASS1`:

> "You are a systematic review data extractor. For each field, first cite the numbered sentence units that carry
> the evidence, then state the value. Cite before you answer, and never copy text — cite unit numbers only."

The per-class contracts, `_CLASS_CONTRACT`, each printed once, at the top of its class block (keys shown as they
render: `field_name`, `unit_indices`, `inference`, `reasoning_steps`, `step`, `criteria_application`, `value`):

> **STATED** — "For each field below: cite at least one unit that asserts the value, then state the value."
> `{"field_name": "...", "unit_indices": [12, 13], "value": "..."}`

> **INFERABLE** — "The paper contains material that fixes these values but does not state them. For each field
> below: cite at least one unit carrying that material, then declare the inference in 1–3 sentences naming the
> step from the cited evidence to the value, then state the value." … "If a cited unit states the value OUTRIGHT
> and you made no inferential step, put the literal `DIRECTLY_STATED` in `inference` instead of inventing one.
> The citation is still required — only the declaration changes."

> **JUDGMENT** — "These values are syntheses that no single passage states. For each field below: reason in
> steps, where EVERY step either cites at least one unit or is explicitly marked as applying the codebook
> criteria with no textual basis claimed; then state the value. A step's `unit_indices` takes bare integers,
> exactly like a field's — `[7]`, never `[S7]`."
> `{"field_name": "...", "reasoning_steps": [{"step": "...", "unit_indices": [7]}, {"step": "...",
> "criteria_application": true}], "value": "..."}`

The line printed after **every** field block, `_escape_line`:

> `*Nothing citable for this field?* {"field_name": "...", "unit_indices": [], "value": "NO_EVIDENCE_LOCATABLE"}`

The general section, in `build_pass1_prompt`:

> "## Escape and absence — these are two different things
> - **NO_EVIDENCE_LOCATABLE** — you searched and could locate nothing in this paper to cite for the field. Emit
>   it as the value with an EMPTY citation list. It is a statement about your search, so it never carries a
>   citation.
> - **N/A, NA, NOT FOUND, NOT REPORTED, NOT_FOUND, NR** — the paper itself does not report the item. That is a
>   claim about the paper's text, so it is a value like any other and REQUIRES at least one citation showing
>   where the paper would have reported it and does not."

`_sentinel_rule`:

> "**The sentinel rule.** A sentinel such as NR is a claim about the paper and must cite the sentence stating
> that absence. If no such sentence exists, output NO_EVIDENCE_LOCATABLE."

and the one sentence that applies to every class:

> "Never return a value of any other kind without at least one citation."

followed by `_worked_example` (three cases on an invented field, `funding_source`), which ends: "Case 1 is the
one that gets missed. If you find yourself about to write a sentinel with an empty citation list, the answer you
want is case 1."

Each field block itself is rendered by `engine/agents/extractor.py`, `_build_field_block`, from the codebook
entry: the definition, then `*Instruction:* <the codebook's instruction>`, value definitions, decision criteria,
examples.

### The attempt-2 feedback template

`engine/elicitation/prompts.py`, `build_feedback_block`. It is appended to the end of the prompt string, after
the numbered paper text (`engine/elicitation/pipeline.py`, `pass1_prompt`: `build_pass1_prompt(...) + feedback`).

> "## Your previous response did not meet the contract on {n} field(s)
>
> Each one below shows what you returned and what its contract requires. Fix these, and re-emit EVERY field in
> the schema — this is a full replacement response, not a patch."

then, per failing field:

```
- **{name}** [{CLASS}]
    you returned value: {echo, capped at 200 chars with "…[truncated]"}
    with unit_indices: [...]
    indices that did not resolve: [...]              (only if any)
    with inference: {echo}                            (INFERABLE only)
    with {n} reasoning step(s)                        (JUDGMENT only)
    ✗ {CODE} — {requirement text for that code and class}
```

The requirement texts are `_requirement`'s. The three that carry most of this read:

> `VALUE_WITHOUT_CITATION` — "a value was asserted with nothing cited behind it. The contract is: {the class's
> accompaniment}. If nothing in this paper can be cited for it, the value must be NO_EVIDENCE_LOCATABLE with an
> EMPTY citation list."

> `STEPS_MISSING` — "a JUDGMENT field needs `reasoning_steps`, not a bare value with a citation. The contract is:
> reason in steps where EVERY step either cites at least one unit or sets criteria_application: true, then the
> value."

> `INFERENCE_MISSING` — "an INFERABLE field needs a declared inference: cite at least one unit, then declare the
> inference in 1–3 sentences (or the literal DIRECTLY_STATED if a cited unit states it outright), then the value."

The feedback lists failing fields only. It says nothing about the fields that passed beyond "re-emit EVERY field".

### The rule choosing which attempt is stored

`engine/elicitation/terminal.py`, `accept_attempt`:

```python
    if second is None:
        return first, 1
    n1 = n_contract_unmet(terminal_states(first, codebook), codebook)
    n2 = n_contract_unmet(terminal_states(second, codebook), codebook)
    return (second, 2) if n2 < n1 else (first, 1)
```

> "Attempt 2 wins only on STRICTLY fewer CONTRACT_UNMET fields. A tie keeps attempt 1"

`engine/elicitation/pipeline.py`, `elicit`: attempt 2 runs "only if attempt 1 left a field failing"; "Two attempts
at most". The accepted attempt is taken whole: there is no per-field mixing across attempts.

### What the checker enforces that the class text does not say

`engine/elicitation/contracts.py`, `check_entry`:

    else:

### The two concentrations, tied to text

**(1) STATED fields whose instruction says "record NR".** The codebook instructions, as rendered into the field
block:

- `comparison_to_human`: "If no comparison was made, record "NR""
- `sample_size`: "Enter the number only, or NR"
- `secondary_outcomes`: "Enter NR if only one outcome reported"
- (`country`, INFERABLE: "If unclear, record "NR"")

Each is followed immediately by the escape line, and the sentinel rule is in the general section further down.
So at the field the model reads an unconditional instruction to write `NR`, and at a distance a rule that `NR`
must cite "the sentence stating that absence" — which, when a paper simply made no comparison, does not exist.
The contract's answer for that case is the escape token; the field's own instruction names the sentinel.

What the model wrote, where it is on disk (the accepted attempt's raw response; `rd1_attempts.json`,
`accepted_attempt_failing_entries`): on `ra_12g`, the STATED entries that failed `VALUE_WITHOUT_CITATION` in an
accepted attempt are `"value": "NR", "unit_indices": []` on `comparison_to_human` (papers 1, 7, 8, 9, 10),
`sample_size` (8, 10), `primary_outcome_value` (8) and `secondary_outcomes` (8), and one `"Original Research"`
with an empty list (`study_type`, paper 10). Across all classes, 9 of the 19 `VALUE_WITHOUT_CITATION` entries in
accepted attempts carry an absence sentinel. This is ELICIT-DESIGN-01's F1 ("`NR` was used 23 times, 19 of them
uncited", quoted in `_escape_line`'s docstring), still present after Ruling 2's three countermeasures, and
located: it is at the fields whose instruction asks for it.

**(2) JUDGMENT fields.** Two codes, two texts.

- `VALUE_WITHOUT_CITATION` (9 on first attempts: all five JUDGMENT fields of paper 4, and one each on papers 1,
  3, 5 and 6). The JUDGMENT sentence offers criteria application as a complete alternative for every step and
  never says that at least one step must cite. Four JUDGMENT entries with this code are on disk, in accepted
  attempts (papers 1 and 6, first attempt; papers 3 and 5, second attempt). Each is one reasoning step, marked
  `criteria_application`, citing nothing: the response the sentence permits, refused by the checker under the
  general line "Never return a value of any other kind without at least one citation". The feedback's
  requirement text for this code repeats the same either/or.
- `STEPS_MISSING` (15 on first attempts: every JUDGMENT field of papers 3, 5 and 7, five each — the code comes
  by the block, not by the field). Paper 7's five are on disk, each answered in the INFERABLE shape
  (`unit_indices`, `inference`, `value`) with real citations. The JUDGMENT shape is printed once, at the head of
  the class block; the only JSON shape printed at each JUDGMENT field is the escape line's flat `unit_indices` /
  `value` object. *CC's judgement:* the shape a field must take is stated once per class and the flat shape is
  restated twenty times, and when the model takes the wrong shape it takes it for the whole block. Papers 3 and
  5 lost their first attempt to the second; that raw text is not on disk, so their shape is not known.

## Answer in the ruling's categories

- **"concentrated where the prompt states the contract weakly (prompt content)"** — **yes, for first-attempt
  failures.** 37 of 46 on `ra_12g` are at three STATED fields whose instruction tells the model to record `NR`,
  and at the JUDGMENT class, whose sentence permits the all-criteria response the checker refuses. The September
  feedback-retry smokes have the same JUDGMENT concentration (10 of 15) on three papers.
- **"spread evenly (model)"** — **no.** Seven fields never failed a first attempt in eleven papers; one failed
  eight times.
- **"is the retry simply doing its job"** — **not simply.** It does its job on what it was built for: it fixed 39
  of 46 on `ra_12g` and 22 of 22 in September. On `ra_12g` it also broke 55 passing fields, 33 of them INFERABLE,
  and lost three whole responses' worth of INFERABLE and JUDGMENT entries. The strict-inequality rule is what
  stands between that and the store, and it held on 6 of 6 net-worse or tied attempts. Where a second attempt
  was accepted, 8 passing INFERABLE fields were given up for 26 repairs.

## PROPOSED class and package

CC proposes; the PI rules. **PROPOSED: Class 2, package P1, pin-affecting**, for the prompt content — the same
class and package 12g-RA-B proposed for RB-3, now with a located cause. Class 2 because nothing stored is wrong:
a field that fails its contract is recorded as `contract_unmet`, loudly, with its codes. What it costs is yield
(28 of 220 fields ended `contract_unmet` on `ra_12g`) and time (a second Pass-1 call on 10 of 11 papers).

Options. All but (e) change what the elicited run sends, or the codebook hash the pin includes, so each is
**pin-affecting** and lands before the freeze smoke records the binding pin (R539). None can be shown to help
without a model run; this read only locates where the failures are.

- (a) **Resolve the `NR` collision at the three STATED fields (and `country`).** Either in the codebook
  instructions ("if no comparison was made" → the escape token) or by a sentence in the STATED contract that
  an instruction to record a sentinel is subject to the sentinel rule. A codebook edit moves the codebook
  hash; a prompt edit moves the prompt hash.
- (b) **State the JUDGMENT contract as the checker enforces it:** at least one step must cite a unit; criteria
  application alone is the escape. One sentence in `_CLASS_CONTRACT` and the matching `_requirement` text.
- (c) **Restate the class shape at each field**, or print the escape line in the class's own shape, for
  `STEPS_MISSING`. Larger prompt; CC's judgement on the cause is the weakest of the three.
- (d) **Change the retry:** drop it; or keep it and have the feedback name what must be preserved. The
  whole-class omissions are not explained, so a change here would be aimed at an unlocated cause.
- (e) **Keep both attempts' raw Pass-1 responses in telemetry** (the rejected one is discarded today). Not
  pin-affecting. Class 3, P7. It is what would have answered "undetermined" above.

CC's PROPOSED choice: **(a) and (b) in P1, before the freeze; (e) in P7; the retry left as built** with its
acceptance rule, which is doing the protecting. (c) and (d) only if a rehearsal after (a) and (b) still shows
them. R563 holds that "E-RETRY-EXP is not opened"; nothing here proposes opening it.
